ShopWarda · Database Design 02

MongoDB schema designed around commerce invariants—not page forms

Products own bounded variants and media; carts reference live variants; orders copy immutable snapshots. There is intentionally no Customer collection.

Embed what changes togetherVariants, option definitions, and product media are edited atomically inside Product.
Reference what grows independentlyCategories, carts, orders, messages, subscribers, events, and logs are separate collections.
Snapshot commercial historyOrder names, SKUs, options, images, prices, address, and totals never depend on future product edits.
Archive instead of deletePublished products, categories, and orders keep stable history and URLs.

6. Lifecycles and invariants

State transitions happen in services; clients never assign arbitrary statuses.

Enforced server-side
Product
draft→active→archived
Cart
active→convertedorexpired
Order
new→processing→shipped→delivered
Payment
COD due→collected·proof uploaded
1
Unique variant combinationNo two variants may select the same option values.
2
One default active variantEvery active product has exactly one purchasable default.
3
Integer money onlyPrices and totals are non-negative piasters; compare-at exceeds price.
4
Stock never below zeroConditional transaction updates reject overselling.
5
Checkout ignores client totalsServer rebuilds totals from current variants and shipping settings.
6
Orders are never deletedCancellation appends history and restores stock idempotently.
7
Category trees cannot cycleA category cannot parent itself or one of its descendants.
8
Proof assets are privateMIME, size, and signed access are enforced server-side.
9
Public analytics contain no PIIFunnel events use anonymous IDs; order/customer data stays in Orders.

This model supports colors, sizes, future option types, category routes, live carts, image replacement, order history, Egyptian shipping, and InstaPay proof without creating a customer-account system.
